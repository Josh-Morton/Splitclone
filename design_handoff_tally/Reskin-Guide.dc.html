Tally (formerly SettleUp)
Reskin Guide
From the current dark, dense build to the light, wave-led Tally target state
Read this first — scope statement
This document describes a visual reskin and rename only. No feature is added, removed or altered. Screen inventory, navigation graph, routes, state shape, split algorithms, currency handling, permissions, sync/offline behaviour, copy semantics and data model are all identical between the two builds. Anything below that looks like a product change is a presentation change to an existing element. If an implementer finds a behavioural difference implied anywhere in this document, treat it as a documentation error and keep the existing behaviour.
1. The two states

Current state (what is built today): the dark SettleUp prototype. Near-black navy background (#0E1521), dark card surfaces, light text, blue accent, dense list rows, tight 5–8px corner radii, system emoji for categories, static page headers, no decorative motif.

Target state (what we are moving to): Tally. Warm off-white background, white cards, deep teal-ink text, a single switchable accent (teal by default), a hand-drawn wave motif used as the brand's signature device, rounded/pill geometry, a consistent Twemoji SVG icon pack, and scroll-responsive headers.

Current — dark SettleUp
Target — Tally
2. Rename

The product is now called Tally. Every user-facing instance of "SettleUp" becomes "Tally": the launch wordmark, the onboarding success toast ("Welcome to Tally"), the seeded demo email domain (josh@tally.app) and the CSV export filename prefix (tally-<space>.csv). Note the one deliberate exception: the action "Settle up" (the button and the settle sheet title) is a verb phrase, not the brand, and must stay.

The app icon has been removed from the launch screen — the wordmark now stands alone — because a new logo is being designed separately. When the logo lands it goes back above the wordmark inside the hero.

The accent is exposed as a tweakable prop (accentColor) with curated swatches — teal #2FB3AC (default), coral #F2635C, amber #F2A03D, blue #5B8DEF, violet #7A5CF0, green #12A87A. Derived tokens --primaryD (accent darkened 24) and --bluebg (accent at 14% alpha) are computed from it, so changing one value re-themes buttons, links, active tabs and the balance hero together. Category tint/foreground pairs are unchanged in hue but now sit on light surfaces.

4. Typography

Roboto → Nunito (400/600/700/800/900), a rounded humanist sans that carries the friendlier tone. The scale is broadly unchanged, with two deliberate increases: screen titles go 22–24px/800 → 26px/900 with -0.8px tracking, and the balance hero figure goes 42px/800 → 46px/900 with -1.4px tracking. Labels stay 11–12.5px uppercase 700–800. Body/list text stays 13–15px.

5. Shape, depth and the wave motif
  • Radii: controls, chips and inputs 14px; cards, sheets and tiles 20px; the balance hero 26px; all primary and secondary action buttons are fully pill (999px).
  • Still zero shadows and zero gradients. Separation comes from flat surface colour, 1px hairlines (--line) and 2px outlines on outlined buttons.
  • The wave motif is the new brand device. Hand-drawn-looking stroked SVG paths (6–7px round-cap white strokes at 25–50% opacity) sit inside accent-filled blocks. It appears in three places: the welcome hero (animated), the home balance card (static, top-right bleed), and the curved wave edge where the welcome hero meets the page. Waves are decorative only — never interactive, never carrying information.
  • Phone shell: flat black bezel → flat deep-teal (#173F4A) bezel, so the frame belongs to the palette.
  • Tab bar: dark translucent → light translucent white with a blur, accent-coloured active item.
6. Iconography

Category and activity glyphs were platform emoji rendered as text, so they changed appearance per OS and per font. They are now the Twemoji 15.1 flat SVG pack, loaded from jsDelivr and rendered as <img> elements sized to 58% of their tinted tile. The mapping is unchanged in meaning: groceries 🛒 1f6d2, rent 🏠 1f3e0, utilities 💡 1f4a1, eating out 🍝 1f35d, transport 🚗 1f697, household 🧴 1f9f4, entertainment 🎬 1f3ac, other 💳 1f4b3, settlement 🤝 1f91d, unknown 🧾 1f9fe, recurring 🔁 1f501. Implementation note: the image elements are constructed in the logic layer and passed to the template as values, not written as raw src holes in markup — a raw hole causes the browser to request the unresolved placeholder string during streaming. Before production, vendor the eleven SVGs locally instead of hitting the CDN.

7. Screen-by-screen presentation changes
Welcome / sign-in
Was: centred icon + wordmark + tagline on a plain background, form below. Now: a 360px accent hero with four animated drifting wave lines (14s alternate loop), a 42pt/900 white wordmark, tagline, and an SVG wave that curves the hero into the page background. Content rises in with a 60–270ms stagger. App icon removed. The email field, "Email me a sign-in code" pill, divider and "Skip — explore the demo household" secondary pill are unchanged in function and order.
Home
The balance block moved from a white card with dark text to a full-bleed accent wave card: white 46pt figure, white pill primary ("Add expense") and ghost-outline pill secondary ("Settle up"). Sync chip, upcoming-bill row and recent-activity card are unchanged in logic. Two presentation-level additions reuse data already computed for Reports: a "Spending in June" category breakdown with bars, and a Recurring bills shortcut row. Neither introduces new data or endpoints.
Expenses / Shopping list / Reports
Static titles became sticky headers that collapse on scroll (see §8). Row content, grouping by date, totals and actions are unchanged; only tile radii, icon rendering and type weights differ.
Detail, sheets, onboarding, invite, settings
Inherit the token, radius, pill-button and icon changes only. Field order, validation, sheet heights and flows are untouched. The wave hero has not yet been extended to OTP / onboarding / invite — that is outstanding work, not a decision.
8. Motion spec
  • Wave drift: welcome hero decoration translates -160px over 14s, linear, infinite alternate. Purely ambient.
  • Rise-in: wordmark, tagline and form block fade up 18px over 500ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1), delays 60/130/200ms.
  • Header collapse: on Home, Expenses, Shopping list and Reports the sticky header responds to scroll position. Progress k = scrollTop ÷ min(44px, available scroll range), clamped 0–1. Title font-size interpolates 26px → 18px; subtitle opacity 1 → 0 and max-height 20px → 0; header padding tightens 10/12px → 5/6px; a --line hairline fades in past k>0.5. Transitions are 160–180ms linear. The header minimises, it never disappears — the title and account controls stay reachable at all scroll positions.
  • No other new animation. Existing sheet, fade, pop and spin keyframes are retained as-is.
9. Explicitly unchanged

Routes and navigation stack; tab structure (Home / Expenses / List / Reports) and the floating add button; split methods (equal, exact amounts, percentage, shares, salary-proportional) and their rounding/remainder distribution; multi-payer expenses; debt simplification; settlements and payment recording; recurring bills including pause and run-now; shopping list with check, clear and convert-to-expense; offline mode, pending-sync states and the optimistic pending flag; activity log and undo-delete; CSV export contents; onboarding (email → OTP → name → salary → space), invite by code or link, and all seeded demo data. Nothing in the reskin depends on a backend change.

10. Outstanding before handoff

  • Extend the wave hero treatment to OTP, onboarding and invite screens.
  • Vendor the Twemoji SVGs locally; drop the CDN dependency.
  • Confirm the final accent (teal is the current default; five alternates are wired).
  • Accessibility pass on the accent-on-white pairs and on white text over the accent hero (currently ~3.1:1 for the 46pt figure — acceptable at that size, verify for smaller labels).
